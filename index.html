Smart Priority System

The extension decides what to fill based on the client's actual data

1

🩻 Body Chart HIGHEST

If the client has circled body areas on the body chart, those areas' muscles are used as the Treatment Area — overriding templates completely.

2

📋 Main Complaint text

If the client has written their complaint (e.g. "lower back pain, worse with sitting"), keywords are detected and mapped to the corresponding muscles.

3

📂 Template Slots A–E FALLBACK

If neither body chart nor complaint is detected, the current slot template is used. After every Done ✅, the slot auto-rotates: A→B→C→D→E→A.
