How It Works

  1. Install NoteFlow from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open your supported BookRelax Work page.
  3. Review and accept the in-extension privacy disclosure.
  4. Create or edit your note templates in slots A-E.
  5. Start auto-fill when you are ready to process treatment notes.

Who This Is For

NoteFlow is for massage therapists, remedial massage practitioners, and massage clinic teams who already use BookRelax and want faster treatment-note documentation. It is not a replacement for BookRelax, clinic scheduling software, or full practice management software. It is a focused Chrome extension for BookRelax treatment notes.

Important Privacy and Product Notes

NoteFlow may read supported BookRelax page content such as appointment details, treatment duration, body chart markers, complaint text, symptoms, and treatment-note fields when needed for note generation. This data is used for the extension's treatment-note workflow. License verification and referral registration are sent securely to Google Apps Script.

NoteFlow is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by BookRelax.
