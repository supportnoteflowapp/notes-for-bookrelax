Why Massage Therapists Search for SOAP Note Tools

Massage therapists and remedial massage clinics often need a faster way to write professional treatment notes after back-to-back appointments. Common searches include massage therapy SOAP notes, SOAP notes for massage therapists, massage treatment notes software, and massage SOAP note generator.

NoteFlow is different from a full practice management system. It is a focused Chrome extension for therapists who already use BookRelax and want to reduce repetitive typing inside the BookRelax treatment-note interface.

Features That Match Massage Documentation Workflows

Treatment note templates

Save reusable wording for 30, 45, and 60 minute massage sessions and rotate between template slots.

Body chart notes

Use supported body chart selections and body-map findings to help prepare treatment area wording.

Client record consistency

Keep repeated clinical wording more consistent across BookRelax treatment note fields.

BookRelax Treatment Note Fields

NoteFlow is designed around BookRelax treatment note fields such as Examination, Symptom, Treatment Area, Treatment Method, Outcome, and Comment. These fields can support a SOAP-note style massage documentation workflow, depending on how your clinic writes notes.

Important: NoteFlow assists with documentation. The therapist remains responsible for reviewing every note and ensuring it accurately reflects the actual treatment provided.
